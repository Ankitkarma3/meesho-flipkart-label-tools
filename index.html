Why Use a Label Crop Tool?

Managing high volumes of parcel dispatches is a daily reality for ecommerce merchants across India. Whether you operate on Meesho, Flipkart, or both marketplaces simultaneously, printing labels efficiently is critical to packing accuracy and meeting daily courier cut-off times.

Eliminate Unnecessary Areas

Default seller portal downloads frequently bundle excess white space, customer tax invoices, or packaging slips. Cropping removes unused layout margins so only essential delivery info is prepared.

Prepare for Thermal Printing

Direct thermal printers (standard 4×6 inch or 100×150 mm rolls) require exact dimension alignment. Cropping isolates the printable parcel sticker so it prints without shrinking or distortion.

Save Manual Editing Time

Manually capturing screenshots or editing PDFs in desktop software for dozen or hundreds of orders is labor-intensive. Specialized label cropping significantly speeds up repetitive packaging prep.

Convenient Browser-Based Workflow

Access tools directly inside modern web browsers on laptops or warehouse dispatch workstations without installing bulky image editing applications or paying costly software subscriptions.

Meesho Label Cropping

When you receive and accept orders through the Meesho Supplier Panel, the platform produces a downloadable dispatch document. In standard settings, Meesho packages the customer tax invoice and the logistics parcel label together on an A4 layout. The actual courier label section—containing the tracking AWB number, pickup barcode, recipient postal address, routing hub code, and supplier return information—typically occupies only a portion of the complete page.

For merchants utilizing dedicated 4×6-inch thermal barcode printers, feeding an uncropped multi-part document leads to formatting errors. The printer may shrink the entire A4 page down into the small sticker label, rendering the barcode lines too thin for delivery personnel scanners to read. Alternatively, it may print blank overflow paper. This is why sellers actively seek to crop Meesho shipping labels prior to sending orders to the print queue.

The basic workflow for cropping a Meesho label involves three straightforward actions:

  1. Download Order Documents: Export your pending dispatch orders from the Meesho Supplier Panel in PDF format or as high-resolution images.
  2. Isolate the Shipping Label: Use a dedicated tool like the Meesho Label Crop Tool available for free on eToolMitra. The tool automatically detects or lets you specify the exact boundaries of the parcel tag, cleanly excluding extraneous borders and invoice text.
  3. Review and Print: Verify that the courier barcode, shipping partner name (such as Shadowfax, Delhivery, Xpressbees, or Ecom Express), and destination pin code are intact and sharp before sending the cropped file to your thermal roll or self-adhesive sheet printer.

Key file considerations include maintaining a minimum resolution of 203 DPI (dots per inch) for thermal heads, avoiding lossy compression artifacts around linear 1D barcodes, and checking that the aspect ratio remains approximately 2:3 to prevent stretching the barcode bars out of scanning tolerance. Flipkart Label Cropping

Sellers operating on the Flipkart Seller Hub (Flipkart Marketplace) generate shipment invoices and logistics slips whenever orders enter the "Ready for Dispatch" state. Flipkart's system generates logistics documents designed for Ekart Logistics and partnered 3PL delivery providers. Similar to other marketplaces, the generated PDF file frequently incorporates the detailed customer tax invoice along with the parcel shipping label on a single layout.

Placing full-sized invoice sheets on the outer surface of a parcel is inefficient, wastes paper, and exposes internal invoice data. By choosing to crop Flipkart shipping labels, warehouse operators can cleanly segregate the outward-facing Ekart shipping label from the invoice. The invoice can be placed securely inside the parcel pouch, while the compact cropped label is affixed cleanly to the corrugated exterior box or tamper-evident courier flyer bag.

The basic steps for cropping Flipkart labels follow a systematic approach:

  1. Generate Flipkart Shipping Documents: In your Flipkart Seller Hub dashboard, navigate to Orders > Active Orders and download the combined shipping labels for your packed orders.
  2. Crop in Browser: Open the Flipkart Label Crop Tool on eToolMitra. Upload your downloaded file to isolate the Ekart delivery label with its prominent tracking barcode, delivery hub acronym, buyer pin code, and seller return address.
  3. Verify Barcode Integrity: Ensure that the Ekart barcode and item identification numbers have not been cropped too closely to the edge. Maintaining a slight white margin (quiet zone) around the barcode is necessary for optical scanner cameras to capture the code accurately at the sorting hub.

When preparing Flipkart shipments, common file formats include PDF and PNG/JPEG image exports. Always aim for lossless conversion so that small typography, such as hazardous material declarations or fragile warnings, remains legible.
