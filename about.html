About Shipping Label Tools

An independent informational resource and practical reference site created to help online sellers understand shipping-label cropping, thermal printing, and dispatch efficiency.

Our Purpose & Mission

Shipping Label Tools was created to address a common daily challenge faced by ecommerce sellers, warehouse operators, and home entrepreneurs across India: how to quickly and cleanly prepare carrier-scannable shipping labels from multi-page marketplace downloads.

Marketplace seller dashboards frequently combine legal customer tax invoices and logistics shipping slips on single A4 PDF pages. When merchants transition to fast, inkless 4×6-inch direct thermal printers, uncropped files cause paper waste, dense unreadable barcodes, and dispatch delays.

Our mission is to provide:

  • Clear, Honest Educational Content: In-depth technical guides explaining DPI resolutions, thermal print settings, and barcode quiet zones.
  • Practical Dispatch Checklists: Step-by-step instructions to prevent courier pickup rejections and Return to Origin (RTO) errors.
  • Direct Links to Free Tools: Reliable, contextual access to the free online cropping utilities provided by eToolMitra.

Online Tools Provided by eToolMitra

This website does not run proprietary backend processing servers or require you to upload private shipping labels to our servers. Instead, we link directly to the specialized, free web utilities hosted on eToolMitra:

  • Meesho Label Crop Tool on eToolMitra: Automatically isolates and crops Meesho parcel slips for 4×6-inch thermal labels.
  • Flipkart Label Crop Tool on eToolMitra: Extracts Ekart delivery tags while preserving barcode scanner clarity.
  • eToolMitra Main Portal: A suite of web tools designed for online sellers and web utilities.
