What Is a Flipkart Shipping Label?

A Flipkart shipping label is the logistics tag generated via the Flipkart Seller Hub whenever orders transition into the dispatch pipeline. Primarily handled by Ekart Logistics (Flipkart’s supply-chain wing) or partnered national couriers, the shipping label provides the technical data needed for automated parcel sorting, transportation routing, and final delivery confirmation.

A complete Flipkart logistics label contains several standardized fields:

  • Tracking ID & Barcode: The primary Ekart logistics tracking number encoded into high-clarity 1D linear barcodes.
  • Delivery Route Code: Prominent destination sorting acronyms (such as BLR/WFD or DEL/NOI) indicating the local fulfillment hub.
  • Recipient Information: Delivery customer name, shipping address, city, state, and 6-digit postal PIN code.
  • Order & Item Details: Flipkart Order ID, Tracking AWB, product name, SKU code, and declaration of declared parcel weight.
  • Seller Return Details: Merchant trade name, warehouse GSTIN, and seller return facility address in case the package cannot be delivered.

When downloaded from the seller portal, this delivery slip is typically bundled alongside the tax invoice on a single document.

Why Crop a Flipkart Label?

Printing full-page uncropped Flipkart shipment documents creates practical challenges in warehouse operations:

Standardize on 4×6 Thermal Rolls

Most modern ecommerce fulfillment stations rely on direct thermal label printers. Cropping lets you output exact 4×6-inch labels without awkward shrinking or extra paper roll wastage.

Separate Label from Invoice

E-commerce best practices require placing the tax invoice inside the packaging pouch and the shipping sticker on the outside. Cropping neatly divides the downloaded PDF into appropriate segments.

Prevent Courier Pickup Rejections

When uncropped pages are scaled down onto small stickers, barcode lines can bleed into each other. If courier pickup executives cannot scan the Ekart barcode instantly on their handheld devices, shipments may be delayed or rejected.

Accelerate Bulk Processing

Using a browser tool to crop Flipkart shipping labels lets sellers prepare batches of orders rapidly during peak festive sales and flash promotions.

Common Label Cropping Problems

Here are key technical pitfalls to look out for when preparing Flipkart labels, along with best practices to prevent them:

Challenge Root Cause How to Fix
Illegible Hub Routing Text Aggressive compression making small text blocks fuzzy. Maintain clear 300 DPI vector output so sorting codes (e.g. EKART Hub identifiers) remain crisp.
Cropping the Barcode Edge Setting the crop rectangle too close to the left or right edge of the barcode. Always preserve a 4mm "quiet zone" (pure white space) on either side of the vertical barcode bars. Optical laser scanners need this contrast buffer to read the code.
Accidental Inversion Accidentally rotating portrait files into landscape mode. Ensure the label is oriented vertically (portrait mode) to match standard 4×6" thermal paper feeds.
Including Invoice Text Leaving the upper boundary of the crop frame loose so that tax amounts appear on the outer package. Tighten the crop perimeter to isolate only the logistics panel, protecting internal product pricing and invoice lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I crop a Flipkart shipping label online?

Yes. Using the browser-based Flipkart Label Crop Tool provided by eToolMitra, you can isolate and crop shipping slips directly in your browser without desktop editing software.

Does cropping alter or invalidate the tracking barcode?

No. Cropping simply trims away external margins and invoice text. As long as you do not compress or distort the barcode proportions, the optical tracking barcode remains 100% valid and readable by courier scanners.
