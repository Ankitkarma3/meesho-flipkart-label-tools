1. What Is a Shipping Label?

In online retail, a shipping label (frequently referred to as a courier manifest, waybill, or logistics slip) is an identification document affixed to the exterior packaging of an ecommerce parcel. It tells logistics carriers who sent the parcel, where it must travel, and how it should be handled throughout its transit journey.

Unlike a standard mailing address sticker, an ecommerce shipping label contains computer-readable data:

  • Tracking Air Waybill (AWB) Barcode: Scanned automatically by conveyor sorting systems and courier handheld mobile terminals.
  • Sort Hub & Delivery Zone Identifiers: Short alphanumeric routing strings indicating the exact regional fulfillment centre or last-mile delivery station.
  • Recipient Address & Contact Details: Delivery destination and recipient telephone number for delivery attempts.
  • Shipment Classification: Prepaid vs. Cash on Delivery (COD), order weight, dimensions, and declared parcel value.
  • Return to Origin (RTO) Address: Detailed warehouse contact information in case the package cannot be handed over to the recipient.

2. Why Sellers Crop Shipping Labels

Marketplace platforms like Meesho and Flipkart package dispatch documents in formats tailored for general A4 laser printing. A single PDF page often contains the shipping label occupying the top half or quadrant, while the lower half contains the customer’s commercial tax invoice, item SKU breakdown, and legal declarations.

However, professional ecommerce packing stations predominantly rely on direct thermal barcode printers loaded with 4×6-inch continuous adhesive roll paper. Sending an uncropped A4 page to a 4×6-inch printer causes severe defects:

  • Unusable Downscaling: The printer driver shrinks the entire A4 sheet into the 4×6 label space. As a result, the linear barcode lines compress too tightly together, making them unscannable for courier optical hardware.
  • Paper Waste: Portions of blank document margins get printed on costly sticker rolls.
  • Customer Privacy & Customs Rules: The external surface of the parcel must carry only the delivery routing tag. The invoice belongs inside the parcel pouch. Cropping enables sellers to separate the two files effortlessly.

3. How to Crop a Meesho Label

Cropping your Meesho dispatch documents allows you to produce sharp, carrier-compliant 4×6 labels. Follow this tested workflow:

  1. Export Labels from Meesho Supplier Panel

    Go to the Meesho Supplier dashboard. Select accepted orders under Ready to Ship and click Download Labels to save the seller PDF file to your local computer.

  2. Load into the eToolMitra Meesho Tool

    Open the free Meesho Label Crop Tool on eToolMitra. Select or drop your downloaded PDF or image file.

  3. Frame the Delivery Segment

    Adjust the crop perimeter to enclose the parcel shipping label section. Verify that the courier routing barcode (Shadowfax, Delhivery, Xpressbees, etc.), recipient address, and seller RTO address are fully included with a slight white margin on all sides.

  4. Save and Print

    Download the cropped file and send it to your thermal roll printer with page scaling set to 100% (or "Fit to Printable Area" without margins).

4. How to Crop a Flipkart Label

Flipkart seller documents feature Ekart Logistics barcodes paired with tax invoices. Here is the recommended procedure to crop Flipkart labels:

  1. Generate Invoices on Flipkart Seller Hub

    From your Flipkart Seller Hub account, navigate to Orders > Active Orders, select packed items, and click Generate Invoice & Label.

  2. Open the Flipkart Cropper on eToolMitra

    Visit the Flipkart Label Crop Tool on eToolMitra in your browser.

  3. Isolate the Ekart Delivery Tag

    Position the crop box strictly over the Ekart shipping label. Exclude the separate packing slip and invoice details. Be sure not to clip the destination hub routing acronyms (e.g. BOM/KRP or DEL/OKH).

  4. Confirm Barcode Quiet Zones & Print

    Double-check that the white spacing to the left and right of the vertical barcode bars has not been trimmed off, then print to your thermal label printer.

5. Image Formats for Label Cropping

Choosing the correct file format is crucial for maintaining print clarity:

Format Best For Pros Cons
PDF (Vector) Direct downloads from seller panels Maintains crisp vector text and barcode lines regardless of printer resolution. Requires compatible browser tools for rendering and splitting pages.
PNG Image-based label crops Lossless compression ensures high contrast between black barcode lines and white background. Slightly larger file size than JPEG.
JPEG / JPG Standard mobile photos or quick previews Universal compatibility and small file sizes. Lossy compression can create blur ("mosquito noise") around barcode bars.

6. Image Quality & Barcode Sharpness

A blurry or distorted barcode is the primary cause of shipment handover delays and courier pickup rejections in Indian ecommerce logistics. Keep these technical standards in mind:

  • Minimum Resolution: Target at least 203 DPI (the native resolution of standard warehouse thermal printers like TVS LP 46 Neo, Zebra ZD220, or TSC TE244). For laser printing, 300 DPI is ideal.
  • Barcode Quiet Zone: Every 1D barcode requires a blank white margin on both sides (at least 3mm to 5mm). Without this quiet zone, optical scanners cannot identify where the barcode sequence starts and ends.
  • Avoid Aspect Ratio Stretching: Never manually drag crop corners disproportionately. Stretching the width while compressing the height changes the bar-to-space ratios required by GS1 or Code-128 barcode standards.
  • Avoid High JPEG Compression: If saving as an image, set quality to at least 90% to prevent fuzzy gray artifacts between barcode bars.

7. Printing Considerations

How you print your cropped labels directly impacts operational costs and durability:

Direct Thermal Printers (Recommended)

Uses heat-sensitive roll paper without ink or ribbons. Produces exact 4×6-inch adhesive labels that can be applied immediately to flyers and corrugated boxes. Fast, smudge-proof, and low cost per label.

A4 Laser or Inkjet Sheets

Useful for small or emerging sellers. You can print cropped labels onto A4 self-adhesive sticker sheets (such as 2-label or 4-label pre-cut sheets) or plain paper taped into transparent courier document pouches.

Printer Scaling Settings

In your printer dialog, ensure scaling is set to "Actual Size" or "100%". Selecting "Fit to Page" on an uncropped document causes shrinkage, while on a cropped 4×6 document it ensures crisp edge-to-edge alignment.

Print Darkness & Speed

In thermal printer properties, set the darkness/density setting between 8 and 12 and moderate print speed (3–4 inches per second) to prevent faint lines or thermal burn bleeding.

8. Common Problems & Practical Solutions

Symptom Root Cause Actionable Fix
Courier scanner cannot read barcode Barcode bars are pixelated, blurry, or missing outer white margins. Crop from the original seller PDF via eToolMitra and avoid re-compressing through social chat apps.
Label prints microscopic on 4×6 paper Uncropped A4 document sent directly to thermal printer. Crop the shipping label section out first so only the 4×6 segment is fed to the print queue.
Parcel returned due to unreadable PIN Crop boundary sliced through the destination delivery PIN or buyer phone number. Always verify that the entire address and contact box is inside the crop perimeter before confirming.
Thermal print fades after a few days Low-grade thermal paper exposed to heat, sunlight, or adhesive tape. Use top-coated thermal labels and avoid sticking standard PVC tape directly over thermal print areas.
